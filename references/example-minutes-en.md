## 2. Purpose

This meeting followed the first discussion on September 1. It settled which blocks go on the member area homepage, the launch schedule and how members are told, as the basis for the design and the development plan.

## 3. Discussion Summary

### (a) Homepage blocks

* Jessie Chen explained that the homepage starts with three blocks: My Orders, Points History and FAQ; other features go in the menu.
* Ming Wang proposed putting My Orders at the top, since order status is what members check most.
* David Lee explained that Points History reads from the current points system, which updates once a day, so the page must show the time of the last update.

### (b) Launch schedule

* David Lee explained that development takes about 3 weeks; with the design final by September 15, the area can launch on October 6.
* Jessie Chen noted the long weekend in early October; the launch should not fall on the day before it.

### (c) Member notices

* Ming Wang proposed an email to members on launch day and a notice on the website homepage for one week.
* It was raised whether to send text messages as well. Jessie Chen said texts cost more; email goes first, and the open rate decides whether texts follow.

## 4. Agreements

1. The homepage has three blocks, My Orders, Points History and FAQ, with My Orders at the top.
2. The design is final by September 15; the planned launch is October 6.
3. Members are told by email on launch day; texts depend on the open rate.
